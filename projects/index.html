FAQ

Questions & Answers

What services do you offer?

I specialize in a comprehensive range of digital services tailored to elevate your online presence. This includes UI/UX Web Design (Figma, WordPress, Duda, Webflow), Full-Stack Development (HTML, JavaScript, CSS, PHP, MySQL, GitHub), Custom Graphic Design, and Professional Video Editing (DaVinci Resolve, CapCut).

How long does it typically take to complete a website project?

Project timelines vary depending on the scope and complexity. A standard business website or portfolio usually takes around 1 to 2 weeks from initial wireframing and design to custom coding, testing, and final launch.

Will my website be mobile-friendly and optimized for search engines?

Absolutely! Every website I build is fully responsive, ensuring seamless performance and visual appeal across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. Additionally, I integrate technical and on-page SEO best practices to help improve your search engine rankings and drive organic traffic.

Can you update or redesign my existing website?

Yes, I can revamp your current site to give it a modern aesthetic, improve user experience, upgrade its performance, or migrate it to a different platform like WordPress, Webflow, or Duda.

What do you need from me to get started on a project?

To kick things off smoothly, it helps to have a clear idea of your brand goals, preferred color schemes or design references, any existing logo/branding assets, and your content (text and images). If you don’t have these ready yet, we can collaborate during the discovery phase to build them out!
